# Academic standing

Students remain in good academic standing with a cumulative GPA of 2.0 or above. A cumulative GPA below 2.0 triggers academic probation. Students on probation should meet with an academic adviser and follow the improvement plan issued by the school.

# Degree completion

The Bachelor of Science degree requires at least 120 earned credits, completion of all required programme courses, and a cumulative GPA of 2.0 or above. Transfer and substituted courses must be approved in the degree audit before they can satisfy a requirement.

# Course registration

Students must meet published prerequisites before registering for an advanced course. Departmental permission and a timetable conflict override do not waive a prerequisite unless the department records an approved exception.

# Assessment and integrity

Students must submit their own work. Teaching staff may provide feedback, conceptual explanations, and practice material, but completing assessed work for another student is not permitted.
