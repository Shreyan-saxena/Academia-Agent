# Literature review workflow

A literature review begins with a focused research question and transparent inclusion criteria. Search more than one scholarly database, record search strings and dates, screen abstracts before full texts, and group findings by method, population, and outcome rather than by paper alone.

# Evaluating a paper

For each paper, identify the research question, design, sample, measures, main result, limitations, and relation to other evidence. A citation count is not a quality score; assess venue, methodology, transparency, and the limits of the inference.

# Citations

Keep a stable record for each source, including authors, title, year, publication venue, persistent identifier, and access date where required by the style guide. Verify citation details against the original record before submitting a bibliography.
