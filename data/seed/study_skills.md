# Building a study plan

Break a topic into specific learning objectives, schedule short retrieval-practice sessions across several days, and use a low-stakes practice question after each study block. Reviewing errors is more valuable than simply rereading notes because it shows which idea needs another explanation.

# Working through a difficult problem

Start by naming the quantities, constraints, and concept being tested. Write an outline before doing calculations. If you get stuck, identify the first uncertain step, consult a worked example for that concept, then solve a comparable problem without looking at the example.

# Preparing for an assessment

Use the syllabus to identify the assessed outcomes, practise under realistic time limits, and make a concise error log. Ask an instructor or tutor for feedback on your reasoning rather than asking someone else to complete an assessed answer.
