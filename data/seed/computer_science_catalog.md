# CS201 — Data Structures

CS201 introduces linear and non-linear data structures, algorithmic complexity, and testing. It is a required prerequisite for CS401. Students must earn a grade of C or better in CS201 to proceed to CS401.

# MATH202 — Discrete Mathematics

MATH202 covers logic, proof techniques, graphs, and combinatorics. It is a required prerequisite for CS401. Students must earn a grade of C or better in MATH202 to proceed to CS401.

# CS401 — Algorithms

CS401 studies algorithm design, correctness, complexity, graph algorithms, and approximation. Registration requires CS201 and MATH202, each completed with a minimum grade of C. The course is normally offered in the fall term; the department publishes its final timetable and capacity before registration opens.

# CS330 — Database Systems

CS330 covers relational modelling, SQL, transaction processing, and query optimization. Registration requires CS201 with a grade of C or better.
